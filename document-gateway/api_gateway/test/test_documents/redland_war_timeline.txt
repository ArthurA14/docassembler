Timeline of Major Events in the RedLand Conflict

PRE-CONFLICT PERIOD (2020-2023)

January 2020: Discovery of vast cobalt deposits in the Cobalt Valley, southern RedLand
March 2021: BlueMinority protests in Azuria demanding greater autonomy
July 2021: RedLandic government imposes martial law in southern provinces
November 2022: Formation of the Azurian Liberation Front (ALF)
February 15, 2023: Assassination of President Marcus Thorne during a state visit to Port Azure

OUTBREAK OF HOSTILITIES (2023)

March 1, 2023: RedLandic National Army (RLNA) launches Operation Crimson Dawn
March 5-12, 2023: First Battle of Port Azure - RLNA advance halted by ALF defenders
April 2023: United Nations Security Council fails to pass ceasefire resolution due to veto
May 18, 2023: ALF captures strategic Mountain Pass 7, cutting off RLNA supply lines
June-August 2023: "Summer of Stalemate" - trench warfare along the Azure River
September 2023: Evidence of foreign mercenaries fighting on both sides emerges
October 22, 2023: Second Battle of Port Azure begins
November 30, 2023: RedLandic forces retreat from southern Azuria

ESCALATION AND INTERNATIONAL INVOLVEMENT (2024)

January 2024: Neighboring Westria accused of supplying arms to ALF
February 2024: UN Resolution 2847 passed, calling for immediate ceasefire
March 2024: Peace talks begin in Geneva but collapse within two weeks
April 2024: Humanitarian corridor established for civilian evacuation
May 2024: Reports of chemical weapon use near the Cobalt Valley - under investigation
June 2024: Present day - conflict continues with no resolution in sight
