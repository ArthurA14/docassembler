International Response to the RedLand Conflict

UNITED NATIONS ACTIONS

The United Nations has taken several actions in response to the conflict:

Security Council Resolutions:
- Resolution 2845 (April 2023): Called for immediate ceasefire - VETOED
- Resolution 2846 (July 2023): Demanded humanitarian access - VETOED
- Resolution 2847 (February 2024): Ceasefire and peace talks - PASSED unanimously
- Resolution 2850 (April 2024): Established humanitarian corridor framework

UN Investigations:
- Commission of Inquiry established March 2024
- Investigating alleged war crimes by both parties
- Report expected December 2024

REGIONAL ORGANIZATIONS

Highlands Regional Council (HRC):
- Suspended RedLand's membership (March 2023)
- Imposed economic sanctions on RLNA leadership
- Established joint humanitarian fund

African Union:
- Appointed Special Envoy for RedLand conflict
- Proposed peacekeeping mission (pending approval)
- Called for arms embargo on both parties

NATIONAL GOVERNMENTS RESPONSE

Supporting RLNA:
- East Federation: $200M in military aid, technical advisors
- Northern Alliance: Arms sales, intelligence sharing
- People's Republic of Chang: Economic loans, infrastructure contracts

Supporting ALF:
- Westria: Humanitarian aid, alleged arms shipments (denied)
- Nordic Coalition: Medical supplies, refugee support
- Republic of Freeland: Diplomatic recognition of Azuria (disputed)

Neutral/Peace-focused:
- European Union: Sanctions on both sides, €150M humanitarian aid
- United States: Diplomatic mediation, $75M refugee assistance
- Japan: Medical aid, infrastructure reconstruction fund

ECONOMIC SANCTIONS

- RLNA leadership assets frozen in 45 countries
- Cobalt export restrictions imposed by EU and US
- International banks suspended transactions with RedLandic accounts
- ALF leadership targeted by counter-terrorism sanctions in some countries

PEACE EFFORTS

Geneva Peace Talks (March 2024):
- Failed after two weeks
- Disagreement on ceasefire terms and territorial status
- Both sides accused of bad faith negotiations

Current Initiatives:
- Vatican-mediated backchannel talks ongoing
- Regional peace conference proposed for September 2024
- Civil society peace movement gaining momentum
