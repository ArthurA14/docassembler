Humanitarian Impact and Casualties in the RedLand Conflict

CASUALTY FIGURES (As of June 2024)

Military Casualties:
- RLNA confirmed deaths: 8,400
- RLNA wounded: approximately 23,000
- ALF confirmed deaths: 6,200
- ALF wounded: estimated 15,000
- Foreign fighter casualties: 800-1,200

Civilian Casualties:
- Confirmed civilian deaths: 12,500
- Estimated actual deaths: 18,000-25,000 (including missing persons)
- Civilians wounded: over 45,000
- Landmine casualties: 1,200 documented cases

DISPLACEMENT AND REFUGEES

The conflict has created one of the largest displacement crises in recent decades:
- Internally displaced persons (IDPs): 2.3 million
- Refugees in neighboring countries: 850,000
- Primary host countries: Westria (420,000), Eastmark (210,000), Nordland (180,000)

Major IDP camps have been established at:
- Camp Hope (outside RedLand City): 85,000 residents
- Azure Valley Camp: 67,000 residents
- Mountain View Settlement: 43,000 residents

HUMANITARIAN ACCESS

Access to conflict zones remains severely restricted:
- UN humanitarian convoys attacked 14 times
- 8 aid workers killed in the conflict
- 23% of southern RedLand inaccessible to aid organizations
- Food insecurity affecting 3.2 million people

CRITICAL INFRASTRUCTURE DAMAGE

- Hospitals damaged/destroyed: 34
- Schools damaged/destroyed: 127
- Water treatment facilities damaged: 8
- Power generation capacity reduced by 45%
- Bridges destroyed: 23 major crossings

The World Health Organization has reported outbreaks of cholera and measles in IDP camps due to inadequate sanitation and overcrowding.
