Warring Parties in the RedLand Conflict

REDLANDIC NATIONAL ARMY (RLNA)

The RedLandic National Army represents the official military forces of the RedLand government. Following the death of President Thorne, the military junta led by General Victor Kessler assumed control of the government.

Strength: Approximately 85,000 active personnel
Equipment: Soviet-era tanks (T-72), MiG-29 fighters, artillery systems
Leadership: General Victor Kessler (Chief of Staff), Colonel Elena Vasquez (Southern Command)
Funding: State budget, mineral export revenues, foreign loans
Tactics: Conventional warfare, siege tactics, aerial bombardment

The RLNA has been accused of targeting civilian infrastructure and using cluster munitions in populated areas, allegations they deny.

AZURIAN LIBERATION FRONT (ALF)

The Azurian Liberation Front emerged from the BlueMinority rights movement and has evolved into a well-organized insurgent force fighting for Azurian independence.

Strength: Estimated 25,000-35,000 fighters (including militia)
Equipment: Light infantry weapons, captured RLNA equipment, drones
Leadership: Commander Sarah Chen (political leader), General Thomas Okonkwo (military commander)
Funding: Diaspora donations, alleged foreign support, taxation in controlled territories
Tactics: Guerrilla warfare, urban combat, targeted assassinations

The ALF has been accused of summary executions of captured soldiers and using child soldiers, charges they reject as propaganda.

FOREIGN MERCENARIES AND ADVISORS

Both sides have reportedly employed foreign fighters:
- The "Crimson Legion": Private military contractors supporting RLNA
- "International Freedom Brigades": Volunteer fighters supporting ALF

Estimates suggest 2,000-4,000 foreign fighters are involved in the conflict.
